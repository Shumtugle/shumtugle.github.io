Vibecoding is the hobby: describe the thing, let the model write it, then argue with the result until it behaves. Half the work is knowing what to ask for. The other half is refusing the first answer.

What survives that argument ends up here.
===
TITLE: Hark
META: android · v2.9.7
URL: https://shumtugle.github.io/hark/
---
A player for long recordings — lectures, audiobooks, talks. It opens a folder rather than a library, and keeps your position in every file separately. In 2.9.7 the home-screen widget was rebuilt as one piece: cover on the right, everything else sized around it instead of against a measured margin.

TITLE: Scoorohot'78
META: android · game · v1.2
URL: https://shumtugle.github.io/scoorohot/
---
A retro racing game on a road that never ends and is never the same twice: seven countries, traffic both ways, forks, police, weather, night. Everything you see and hear is made by the game itself. A finger on the screen drives; slide to steer, drag down to brake.

TITLE: Clepsydra
META: android · game · v1.01
URL: https://shumtugle.github.io/clepsydra/
---
A round glass flask with a maze inside. Rain falls into the neck; turn the flask with one finger and let the water out of the bottom in one long pour. Every flask is a new maze, and some keep a trouble or two: bubbles to pop or to keep, pearls that open into flowers across the way, a float, a clock, snow, lava.

TITLE: Cat Piano
META: android · music · v1.04
URL: https://shumtugle.github.io/catpiano/
---
A cat, a panda, a fox and a small dinosaur take turns at the keys. The music is written while they play, grown from nine thousand phrases of public-domain keyboard works, and no two pieces are alike. Each musician brings its own colours, hall and instrument; the keys sound under your fingers too.
