Vibecoding is the hobby: describe the thing, let the model write it, then argue with the result until it behaves. Half the work is knowing what to ask for. The other half is refusing the first answer.

What survives that argument ends up here.
===
TITLE: Hark
META: android · v2.9.7
URL: https://shumtugle.github.io/hark/
---
A player for long recordings — lectures, audiobooks, talks. It opens a folder rather than a library, and keeps your position in every file separately. In 2.9.7 the home-screen widget was rebuilt as one piece: cover on the right, everything else sized around it instead of against a measured margin.

META: android · v1.9.12

META: android · v3.327.0

Jot is a compact Android editor for quick image work: draw, crop, add text and shapes, adjust colour, apply filters, remove parts of an image, combine pictures, and save the result.

TITLE: Nixie
META: android . browser
URL: https://shumtugle.github.io/nixie/
---
А small Android browser, built with the platform and nothing else. Nixie draws pages with the Android System WebView and builds its own window around it: one bar, one button, and screens of its own for everything a browser keeps.
